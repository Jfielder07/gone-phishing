How do I save an email as an .eml file?
  • Gmail: open the email, click the three-dot menu next to Reply, then Download message.
  • Outlook on the web: open the email, click the three-dot menu, then Download (or Save as).
  • Apple Mail: drag the email from your inbox onto the desktop.

Privacy notice: Emails classified as phishing are saved to our database along with their analysis. To generate explanations, part of each phishing email is sent to Google's Gemini API. On this free service, Google may use that content to improve its products and train its AI models, and human reviewers may read it. Emails classified as safe are not saved or sent to Gemini. Please don't upload emails containing passwords, financial details or other sensitive personal information.
